# M01 · Interview Reality — BRIEF

## The setup

You're in a 45-minute onsite loop. I'm your interviewer. I slide a marker across the
table and say:

> **"Design the checkout system for Lumo."**

That's it. No requirements doc. No numbers. No hints. The clock is running.

## Your job

Not to produce the "right" architecture — there isn't one. It's to show me how you
*think*: how you turn a deliberately vague prompt into a concrete, defensible design,
out loud, as a conversation.

## How we run it

This is a live mock. **You drive.** I play the interviewer — sometimes helpful,
sometimes deliberately quiet, occasionally throwing a curveball. Afterwards we pull apart
what you did, name the patterns (and the traps you did or didn't fall into), and I show you
what actually separates a *hire* from a *no-hire* on exactly this prompt.

First move is yours.
