or `/whiteboard`, read `learning/TEACHING.md` and the matching `.agents/skills/` skill.

integration, distinguishing existing infrastructure from proposals.

The teaching workflow applies to learning sessions; it does not turn ordinary development
requests into lessons. Preserve existing project conventions and Nathan's work.
